Scout is built to sharpen what everyone wants to blur.

An examined life is not for everyone. That is why you are here.

Set aside two hours. Not ninety minutes. Two hours. The people who got the most from this gave it that.

Scout will sit with you in a conversation that goes somewhere most conversations never reach — following what you say, and sometimes what you don't.

At the end you will receive two things — a portrait written about you, and a Meridian. Both belong to you alone. Download both before you close the window.

While you are here

Your conversation is held privately during your session — like a document that saves itself while you write. If your connection drops or your browser closes, you can return with the same key and Scout picks up exactly where you left off.

Once your documents are delivered and you close this window, the conversation is permanently removed. Nothing is retained. Your inner life passes through this system and leaves no trace.

Your key expires the moment your documents are delivered. It cannot be reused.

What this asks of you

This will only work if you are willing to think carefully, speak plainly, and go into as much detail as the question deserves.

Scout will not judge you. It will not give you advice. It will not tell you what to do. It will listen without agenda and reflect back what it actually hears — including the things you gestured at without quite landing on.

The people who get the most from this are the ones who treat it seriously. Who answer the question that was asked rather than the one that was easier. Who stay with a thought rather than moving past it.

What emerges becomes the foundation of something that will serve you every day. Not a plan. A companion — already in development — that reads your portrait and Meridian every morning and speaks to you from that knowledge. On the days when you cannot hear yourself clearly, it knows who you are at your core and does not forget. The quality of that companion depends entirely on the quality of what you bring today.

One thing to know first

Scout works best when you are in a stable place. If you are currently in a difficult period — or receiving support for your mental health — this is not the right moment. Not because anything is wrong with you. Because this kind of careful self-examination deserves the right conditions. Please speak with your doctor or therapist before proceeding.

If questions about your relationships or personal life arise during the session — Scout will acknowledge them. But some territory deserves a human specialist, and Scout will say so.

When you are ready — enter your key.
