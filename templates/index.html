Technically

Set aside two hours. Not ninety minutes. Two hours. The people who got the most from this gave it that.

Scout will sit with you in a conversation that goes somewhere you always wanted to go, but never had the company — following what you say, and sometimes what you don't.

At the end, it will produce something called a spine — a personal constitution that captures the real version of you, not the performed one. It belongs to you alone.

During your session, your conversation is held in a temporary file — like a document that saves itself while you write. If your connection drops or your browser closes, you can return with the same key and Scout picks up exactly where you left off. The moment your spine is delivered and you close this window, that file is deleted permanently. Nothing is kept after that point. Nothing.

Your key expires the moment your spine is delivered. It cannot be reused.

With truth

This will only work if you are honest. Not performatively honest — not the version of honesty that sounds good. The kind that costs something to say out loud.

Scout will not judge you. It will not give you advice. It will not tell you what to do. It will listen without an agenda, without distraction, and without the need to protect itself from what you say — and it will reflect back what it hears, including the things you did not quite say.

The people who get the most from this are the ones who stop editing themselves. The ones who say the thing they have never said to anyone. The ones who let the silence sit rather than filling it with noise.

What emerges from this session becomes the foundation of something that will serve you every day. Not a plan. Not a to-do list. A companion that knows who you actually are — and speaks to you from that knowledge, every morning, for as long as you use it. The quality of that companion depends entirely on the quality of what you give today.

Scout works best when you are in a stable place. If you are currently experiencing significant mental health difficulties — or if you are in active treatment for a mental health condition — this is not the right moment for this kind of work. Not because anything is wrong with you. Because this process goes deep, and deep is not always what is needed right now. Please speak with your therapist or doctor before proceeding.

If you want to explore sexual or relational complexity — Scout will acknowledge it and note it. But this is territory that deserves a human specialist. Scout will point you there.

When you are ready — enter your key.
